# Superdock vs InfyniDock

By Mahad Kazmi, developer of Superdock. Updated 3 September 2026.

Short answer. InfyniDock makes every window a Dock target, with labels and previews, aimed at people who keep many projects and Spaces open. Choose Superdock if you want Apple's Dock with app tiles, Chrome split by profile rather than by window, and previews for the rest..

InfyniDock makes every window a Dock target, with labels and previews, aimed at people who keep many projects and Spaces open. Superdock keeps app-level tiles that look like Apple's and splits only what needs splitting: Chrome by profile.

We make Superdock. Facts about InfyniDock come from its own website as read on 3 September 2026; "not stated" means the page did not say. Tell us at hello@superdock.app if something is out of date.

## Choose InfyniDock if
You want one dock entry per window across all apps, and a dock that doubles as a window manager.

## Choose Superdock if
You want Apple's Dock with app tiles, Chrome split by profile rather than by window, and previews for the rest.

## Try both
Superdock is free to download and the Chrome profile icons are free for 14 days. Run one dock at a time: two apps watching windows at once can freeze the desktop.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which is better for many VS Code windows?
InfyniDock lists each as a target. Superdock shows one VS Code tile with previews and recent projects in the right-click menu.

Download free The dock is free. Chrome profile icons: 14 days free, then $7.99 once. macOS 14 or later.
