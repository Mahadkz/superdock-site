DockSpark is the closest neighbour: hover Chrome's Dock icon and a panel lets you open a profile or an incognito window. It is a launcher for profiles. Superdock goes further and gives each profile its own tile, with the avatar, its own window list, previews and a running indicator.

Choose DockSpark if

You just want a quicker way to open a profile than Chrome's avatar menu, you are happy with one Chrome icon, and you want it free today. It also covers Safari, Firefox and other browsers.

Choose Superdock if

You want to see which profiles are open, click straight to a profile's windows, right-click for only its windows, and pin a profile as a first-class tile. That needs a dock that owns its tiles.

Try both

Superdock is free to download and the Chrome profile icons are free for 14 days. Run one dock at a time: two apps watching windows at once can freeze the desktop.

Frequently asked questions

Does DockSpark show which profile a window belongs to?

Its site describes a hover panel of profiles to open; it does not describe attributing windows to profiles or per-profile tiles.

Does Superdock create profiles?

No, neither app does. Create them in Chrome; Superdock reads Chrome's profile list.
