By Mahad Kazmi, developer of Superdock. Updated 3 September 2026.

Short answer. uBar is the long-running Windows-taskbar-for-Mac: window buttons, grouping, badges, per-monitor bars, positionable on any edge. Choose Superdock if you want the Mac to still look like a Mac.

## Side by side

We make Superdock. Facts about uBar come from its own website as read on 3 September 2026; "not stated" means the page did not say. Tell us at hello@superdock.app if something is out of date.

## Choose uBar if
You want a taskbar, not a Dock: one button per window, rows of buttons, CPU and RAM on hover, and you do not mind that it looks nothing like macOS.

## Choose Superdock if
You want the Mac to still look like a Mac. Superdock is pixel-matched to Apple's Dock, gives each Chrome profile its own tile without a second Chrome, and the dock itself is free; uBar is $30.

## Try both
Superdock is free to download and the Chrome profile icons are free for 14 days. Run one dock at a time: two apps watching windows at once can freeze the desktop.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does uBar separate Chrome profiles?
Its site describes grouping by app; it does not describe per-profile grouping. Chrome profiles share one process, so a taskbar that groups by app shows one Chrome.

### Which is cheaper?
Superdock's dock is free and the profile feature is $7.99 once for five Macs. uBar Personal is $30 for two Macs.
