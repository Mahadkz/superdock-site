Frequently asked questions

Does Superdock hide Apple's Dock?

Yes, while it runs, and it restores Apple's Dock and its settings when it quits, even after a crash, through a small watchdog.

Does it follow Apple's Dock settings?

Size follows Apple's own slider, magnification can be on or off, and icon style follows the system Default, Dark, Clear and Tinted setting.
