Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026.

Superdock is built to need as little of your data as possible.

What the app collects

The app itself does not send your browsing, your windows, or your profiles anywhere. Everything it does happens on your Mac. Your license is a file stored on your Mac and checked on your Mac; a one-time license is never sent anywhere.

Yearly licenses

If you buy a yearly license, the app contacts our license service near renewal time to fetch your renewed license. That request carries your license id and nothing about your browsing. We use it only to renew your license.

Purchases

When you buy Superdock, our payment provider processes your payment and holds your billing details under their own privacy policy. We receive your email address to send you your license and support.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, and ask us to delete it, at hello@superdock.app. Since we hold little more than your email and license, this is quick.
