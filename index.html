01

macOS sees one Chrome.
You run six.

Every Chrome profile shares one process, so the Dock collapses them into a single icon. Switching between work, clients and personal becomes a guessing game behind one tile.

02
The one thing no dock can do

One icon per profile.

Split Dock draws its own tiles. Each profile gets its own icon with its own avatar, using your real Chrome. No second copy eating memory, no logging in again.

Pin, unpin and drag a profile tile like any app icon. You cannot tell it apart from a normal one.

03
Know before you click

Previews that stay in their lane.

Hover a tile to see its windows before you switch. Editor windows lead with the workspace, not the file, so you land on the right one first time.

Hover a profile and you see only that profile's windows. Nothing from your other identities leaks in.

04
One click, every window

The whole desktop, at a glance.

Click the empty right side of the dock and every window fans out at once. See all your work and jump straight to the one you need.

Click the left side to clear the desktop. Both are yours to set, or switch off, in a tap.
