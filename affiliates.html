Affiliate program

Earn 30% of every Superdock licence you refer. That is $2.40 a sale at the launch price, paid to you by Lemon Squeezy, with no minimum volume and nothing to build.

How it works

  1. Apply. Affiliate accounts are handled by Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record, so you sign up with them and then apply to our programme at superdock.lemonsqueezy.com/affiliates.
  2. Share your link. You get a tracking link for any page on superdock.app. Write a review, record a video, put it in a newsletter, answer a question on a forum: whatever suits your audience.
  3. Get paid. Commission is created on the 1st and 15th of the month and paid on the 14th and 28th, after a 30-day hold, by bank transfer or PayPal in US dollars.

The terms, plainly

Commission30% of the sale
On a $7.99 licence$2.40 to you
Attribution window30 days from the click
CookiesNone. Lemon Squeezy tracks referrals without them.
Who pays youLemon Squeezy, not us
Payout minimum$10
RefundsA refunded order voids its commission

What we ask

Be honest about what Superdock does and does not do. Say that the dock itself is free and that the licence unlocks the Chrome profile icons. Disclose that your link earns you a commission, because most places require it and every reader deserves it. Do not bid on our brand name in paid search, and do not post your link where self-promotion is against the rules.

Beyond that, write what you actually think. A review that names the limitations converts better than one that does not, and we would rather have the honest one.

Questions

Do I get a discount code to share?

No. Referrals are tracked by link only. If a code would genuinely work better for your audience, email us and we will talk about it.

How do I know a sale was mine?

Your Lemon Squeezy affiliate dashboard shows clicks, referrals and earnings. We see the same numbers on our side.

Is there a minimum audience?

No. One good post that helps the right person is worth more to us than a large list that ignores it.
