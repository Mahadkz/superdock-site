Which Mac app affiliate programs are actually worth joining?

By Mahad Kazmi, developer of Superdock. Updated 11 September 2026.

Look for three things: a commission above 20%, a tracking window of at least 30 days, and a merchant of record who pays you directly rather than a developer who promises to remember. Most Mac utilities fail at least one. Superdock pays 30% with a 30-day window, and Lemon Squeezy pays you, not us.

The honest problem with most Mac utility programmes

Mac apps are cheap. A $7.99 or $14.99 one-time purchase does not leave much to share, so many developers skip affiliates entirely or offer 10% and hope. That is rational for them and useless for you: ten sales of a $10 app at 10% is ten dollars.

The programmes worth your time do one of two things. Either the price is high enough that a small percentage is meaningful, which is why you see so many affiliates for $99 subscriptions, or the percentage is high enough to matter at a low price. A programme that offers neither is asking you to work for exposure.

What Superdock pays, and why

30% of every sale. At the $7.99 launch price that is $2.40, and $4.20 when the price returns to $13.99. We can afford it because the app is a one-time purchase with no per-customer server cost, and because a recommendation from someone who actually uses it converts far better than anything we could buy.

Lemon Squeezy handles the money as our merchant of record. They track the referral for 30 days without cookies, create commissions on the 1st and 15th, pay on the 14th and 28th after a 30-day hold, with a $10 minimum, by bank transfer or PayPal. We never touch your payment, which means we cannot forget it.

What to check before joining anything

  1. Read the refund rule. Every honest programme voids commission on a refunded order. One that does not mention refunds has not thought about it.
  2. Ask what happens to your links if the product is discontinued. Nobody enjoys the question; the answer tells you a lot.
  3. Try the product first. A review that reads like an advert converts worse than one that names a flaw.
  4. Check whether there is a free version you can send people to without asking for a card.
