Activate your license

The Dock is free. A license unlocks one icon per Chrome profile. Here is exactly how to turn it on.

If you already bought it

  1. Check your email. After you buy, we email you a small license file called your-name.superdock-license. Look in your inbox (and spam, just in case).
  2. Open Superdock. Click the Superdock icon in your menu bar, then open Settings ▸ License.
  3. Add the file. Click Add license and choose the file from your email. That’s it — your profile icons come back right away.

The license works offline. Superdock never phones home to check it, and it keeps working with no internet.
