# Listicle plan
Rewritten 12 September 2026 from 27 newly mapped SERPs plus the 12 in the first
pass. Supersedes the 12-SERP draft. Companion to `content-clusters.md`,
`comparison-pages-plan.md` and `problem-queries-plan.md`.

## The finding that changes the strategy

My first draft assumed audience slices were the play. **They are not, for us.**

The 27-query sweep found the space splits cleanly in two:

- **Audience-defined queries** ("best mac apps for video editors / project
  managers / writers / traders") are owned by PCMag, Setapp, TechRadar and B2B
  SaaS with real budgets. There is also **no honest Superdock slot** in any of
  them. Do not touch these.
- **Constraint-defined queries** ("under $10", "8GB RAM", "one-time purchase",
  "too many tabs", "work vs personal accounts", "multiple monitors") have weak or
  stale SERPs, **and we qualify on the constraint's own terms** rather than
  needing a stretch.

Publishers write for job titles. That is exactly why the constraint queries are
open. This is the single most useful thing the research produced.

## Two more findings worth obeying

**Several of these SERPs are abandoned, not competitive.** "Best mac apps under
$10" has an **eight-year-old Reddit thread at #1**. "Multiple external monitors"
has a **2023 Lifehacker** at #2. "One-time purchase" and "Setapp alternatives"
both have Reddit threads two to three years old at #1.

**Half these SERPs are Reddit-dominated, and that is a trap as well as an
opportunity.** No article has claimed the slot, but Google is reading the intent
as discussion, so a commercial-feeling page may never rank. Every page that HAS
broken through (theodorehq, orbl, supasidebar, brnsft) reads as an honest
comparison that recommends competitors, including free ones. **A page that only
pitches Superdock will not displace a Reddit thread.**

## Table 4. Page structure

| Section | Content | Words |
|---|---|---|
| TL;DR | Complete answer, top 3 picks named, who each is for | 60 |
| Bias disclosure | First person, first screen. "I build one of these, discount me accordingly" | 40 |
| Scope | What is covered, and explicitly what is not | 40 |
| The apps | 8 to 12 entries: official icon, price, one-line verdict, **one honest limitation each** | 900 to 1,400 |
| Comparison table | One table, after the entries, conditional verdict column | 150 |
| Who should pick what | Reader-sorting: "if you only want X, get Y instead" | 200 |
| FAQ | 5 to 8 visible h3 questions phrased as search queries. Keep FAQPage schema (see note) | 300 to 500 |
| Method footer | Date, what was tested, what was not | 40 |
| Author box | Byline to the profile page | existing |

Target 1,400 to 2,200 words.

## Table 5. Honesty devices, which are the mechanism not the garnish

| Device | Observed in the wild | Our version |
|---|---|---|
| Rank yourself last | Droppy ranks itself 4th of 4 on clipboard managers | In `best-mac-apps-customise-dock-menu-bar`, DockDoor is free and may be the better answer. Say so |
| Omit yourself entirely | Droppy is absent from its own free-apps listicle | **Write one listicle with no Superdock in it.** That is the page others can link to |
| Give a rival an unqualified win | "The Boring Notch is the best free starting point for most people" | "If you only want previews on Apple's Dock, DockDoor is free and open source. Install that instead" |
| Name your bias as the reason a trial exists | "I would say that, which is exactly why the trial exists" | Ours is 14 days and the dock is free forever |
| Conditional verdicts | "#1 if you want X without switching browsers" | Never a bare "best". Always "best if" |

## Caveats
- **No keyword volumes.** Ease is SERP-structure based. Treat as ordinal.
- Many results carry 2026 dates, some after today, which suggests date inflation
  in the index. Treat recent stamps as soft signals; the **stale** entries (2015
  to 2023) are the reliable openings.
- The dock and notch neighbourhood has at least nine indie app sites already
  running this strategy, several of them direct competitors. The uncontested
  ground is the constraint queries, where none of them have appeared yet.
