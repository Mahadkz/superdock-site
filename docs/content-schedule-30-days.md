# 30-day content schedule
Two pieces per day, 60 total. Built 12 September 2026 from the research in
`listicle-plan.md`, `problem-queries-plan.md`, `comparison-pages-plan.md` and
`content-clusters.md`.

## Pairing rule

Each day pairs **one large piece with one small one**. This matters for three
reasons: it keeps daily output realistic, it stops the site reading as a listicle
farm, and it means every large piece ships with a small piece that links to it.

- **Large** (1,400 to 2,400 words): listicles, pillars, comparison pages
- **Small** (800 to 1,200 words): problem-shaped answers, single questions

## Ordering logic

1. **Empty SERPs first.** Seven queries have positions 1 to 3 held entirely by
   forums with no product answering. Cheapest wins available.
2. **Constraint listicles before audience listicles.** Constraint queries are
   open because publishers write for job titles.
3. **Pillars last within a cluster**, once spokes point at them.
4. **The two gated pieces are held back**: the non-running-tiles page waits for
   the feature, any Edge page waits on verifying Edge title attribution.

## Not in the schedule, and why

Video editors, project managers, writers, traders, musicians, streamers: all
publisher-owned SERPs with no honest slot for us. Students: Setapp owns it.
Privacy and anti-fingerprinting: the premise is technically contested. Always on
top, notification badges, hide others, autohide delay: we do not have those
features and writing them would overclaim.
