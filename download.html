How it works

  1. Download and open. Drag Superdock to your Applications folder and launch it.
  2. Grant one permission. Superdock asks for Accessibility once, so it can read your open windows. Nothing else is required to start.
  3. Use it free for 14 days. All features, no account. After the trial the Dock keeps working; a license brings the Chrome profile icons back.

One licence, five Macs

A licence is a one-time purchase and covers up to five Macs you own, with every future update included. Install the app on each Mac and enter the same licence; remove a Mac from the licence at any time in Settings or on the manage page.
