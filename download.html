Download Superdock

macOS 13 or later. Free for 14 days, no account, no card. Every Chrome profile gets its own Dock icon during the trial.

Download for Mac

The download goes live with the first release. It is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens with a double-click — no security warnings.

How it works

  1. Download and open. Drag Superdock to your Applications folder and launch it.
  2. Grant one permission. Superdock asks for Accessibility once, so it can read your open windows. Nothing else is required to start.
  3. Use it free for 14 days. All features, no account. After the trial the Dock keeps working; a license brings the Chrome profile icons back.

Building it yourself

Superdock is free software under the GNU GPL v3. You can build it from source instead of downloading the ready-to-run app — the full source is public. This is your right under the license, and always will be.
