# Download Superdock

Current version: 1.0.1 for macOS 14 or later. Direct download: https://superdock.app/Superdock-1.0.1.dmg (signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized; opens with a double-click).

## How it works

1. Download and open. Drag Superdock to your Applications folder and launch it.
2. Grant one permission. Superdock asks for Accessibility once, so it can read your open windows. Nothing else is required to start.
3. Use it free for 14 days. All features, no account. After the trial the dock keeps working; a licence brings the Chrome profile icons back.

## One licence, five Macs

A licence is a one-time purchase ($7.99 launch price) and covers up to five Macs you own, with every future update included. Buy at https://superdock.app/buy.html. Remove a Mac from the licence any time in Settings or at https://superdock.app/manage.html.
